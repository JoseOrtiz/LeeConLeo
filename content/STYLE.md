# Illustration style

Every picture in the app follows this guide, whether it is drawn by hand in SVG
or generated by an image AI.

## Look

- Flat vector shapes with thick, rounded dark outlines. No gradients, no textures.
- One object per picture, centered, on a plain or transparent background.
- No text, letters or numbers inside pictures.
- Child-friendly proportions: big heads, big eyes, short limbs, round shapes.
- Shading, when needed, is a single darker flat tone of the same color.

## SVG rules

- `viewBox` sized to the picture: `0 0 400 400` for words, `0 0 400 560` for Leo.
- Outlines are 8 units wide, with round joins and caps.
- Only basic elements (`path`, `circle`, `ellipse`, `rect`, `g`) with inline
  attributes. No `<style>`, CSS classes, filters, masks, text or embedded images,
  so `flutter_svg` renders them the same everywhere.
- One element per line, so a file can be edited by hand.
- Word pictures live in `assets/images/words/`, named after the word in lowercase
  ASCII: accents dropped and `ñ` written as `n` (`árbol` → `arbol.svg`,
  `iglú` → `iglu.svg`).
- Grounded objects sit on a soft shadow: an ellipse in `outline` at 15% opacity.

## Leo

Leo is a boy of about five, and the child's guide through the app. His blond hair
is shaped like a lion's mane, because *Leo* comes from *león*.

- **Hair:** pointed locks radiating around the head, in `hair` with a `hairShade`
  inner layer, plus a spiky fringe over the forehead. The hair never covers the chin.
- **Face:** round, with large dark oval eyes and two white highlights each, short
  brows, pink cheeks and a small smile.
- **Clothes:** cream short-sleeved shirt with two buttons, open red vest, brown
  shorts, dark brown boots.
- **Proportions:** the head, including the hair, is about 40% of his height.
